# Originality and content policy

This package was assembled specifically as a visual companion to the modulated-bathymetry research project. It is independent of the manuscript distribution.

## Included material

- Original prose explaining the model, calculations, and limits of the demonstrations.
- An original implementation of the displayed linear systems and spectral matrices.
- Newly computed trajectories, eigenvalue summaries, and diagnostics.
- Original generated GIFs, static posters, workflow artwork, badges, and navigation tiles.
- Project-specific citation metadata and an original permission notice.

The numerical summaries are small JSON outputs of the included equations. No external dataset is imported. The badges are locally drawn SVGs, not downloaded shields. All icons in the prose are ordinary text emoji; no icon image library is bundled.

## Excluded material

No manuscript PDF, manuscript source, LaTeX file, CSV dataset, copied published figure, paper screenshot, externally downloaded animation, third-party source code, vendored dependency, font binary, prior repository history, or nested archive is included.

## Mathematical provenance

The project equations motivate these new explanatory materials. Standard mathematical identities, Fourier methods, and basic numerical operations are implemented and explained directly. No article prose, figure, or external source-code passage was inserted into this package. Originality here concerns the distributed expression, code, and graphics; it is not a claim that standard mathematical methods were invented for this repository.

## Third-party tools

Separately installed numerical libraries, imaging libraries, and system fonts were used to compute and render the assets. They are acknowledged in the reproduction guide and remain subject to their own terms. They are not redistributed as part of this package.
